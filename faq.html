Frequently Asked Questions

This school uses a montessori-like approach that is based on the students specific needs. This school also offers honors courses as well as dual credit.

These classes are considered to be small. Each class of 10 students has two teachers that specialize in different areas of studies.

There is a matrix of criterea that students are expected by the end of the school year. Students are regularly assessed by their teachers and staff to see where their progress is.

A handout and homework packet will be placed in your students backpack explaining in detail at the beginning of the year. No homework is expected during the first two missed days. On the third day and then following, one page of work from the packet will be expected to be completed when students return.

The school will communicate with provided phone and email. Emergency comminications will be sent out quarterly to test the system. If you do not receive these messages, contact the office.
